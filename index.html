What OTR does today

  • It is the stake in OnlyRolls. You roll under a number to win, and bets and payouts run in OTR.
  • It earns more OTR on EMIT. Stake OTR in a single-token EMIT farm and the rewards are paid in OTR too.
  • It is a Folding@home reward. DrDoge pays OTR to people who fold proteins with their computer. See the Fold section below.
  • It is the only way into TFR. TFR can only be bought by swapping OTR, and two staking pools pay TFR to people who stake OTR or TFR.
  • It takes 5% off an OnlyMiners order. Pay with OTR at checkout for the discount.

Why it all comes back to OTR

TFR can only be bought by swapping OTR, and the TFR / OTR pool was burned to the dead address, so OTR is TFR's main liquidity. TFR's dollar price is OTR's price multiplied by that pool's ratio, so the value of OTR sits underneath every TFR you earn.

That is why this route needs no buying: you earn OTR on EMIT, earn TFR by staking that OTR, and earn more TFR by staking the TFR.

Rates fall as more people stake. Rewards are paid in tokens that can lose value, the EMIT farm charges the withdraw fee shown here, and the numbers update every 30 seconds from the chain. Nothing here is financial advice.

Earn OTR by folding proteins

Folding@home lets your computer help with real medical research. DrDoge pays OTR, along with other tokens, to people who finish folding work under a DrDoge username. There is nothing to connect, sign or claim: rewards go to the PulseChain address in your username.

Set it up in seven steps

  1. Get a PulseChain address

    Any PulseChain wallet works. Copy your address from Rabby, MetaMask or Internet Money.

  2. Install Folding@home

    Download the version 8.5 client for Windows, macOS or Linux and let it install.

    Download Folding@home
  3. Open Web Control

    Go to app.foldingathome.org in Chrome, Firefox or Brave. Safari blocks the connection. Your computer shows up under Machines after a few seconds.

    Open Web Control
  4. Set your username

    Click the gear on your machine and enter DrDoge_ followed by your PulseChain address, with no spaces. The builder in the tools makes it for you.

  5. Join the DrDoge team (optional)

    Enter team number 1066815 to appear on the DrDoge leaderboard. You can fold under any team and still earn.

    See the team
  6. Add a passkey

    Request a passkey for your exact username, then paste it into the same settings screen. Without it you earn far fewer points, and your first 10 work units earn less either way.

    Get a passkey
  7. Press Fold

    Set your name and passkey first. Work done before that is credited to Anonymous and earns nothing. Rewards are paid for work units you finish and send back.
